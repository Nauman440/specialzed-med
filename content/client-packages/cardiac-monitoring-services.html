What are cardiac monitoring services?

Cardiac monitoring services record and organize ambulatory ECG data for physician review.Specialized Medical supports short and longer Holter studies, Cardiac Event Monitoring and Mobile Cardiac Telemetry. The ordering physician determines the appropriate test, duration and notification protocol.

The important differences are not limited to wear time. Healthcare organizations should also compare whether the study provides live operational visibility, when clinical findings are presented, how patients receive help and how reports reach the ordering physician.

Operational visibility

LIVE visibility helps protect monitoring time

A prescribed study is useful only when the monitor remains powered, attached, connected and transmitting as expected. For supported live studies, Specialized Medical follows relevant operational indicators and can assist the patient when a problem is identified.

Operational visibility is not the same as a clinical alert. Event and MCT communication of qualifying findings follows the ordering physician's prescribed notification protocol.

Frequently asked questions

Cardiac monitoring service answers

What cardiac monitoring services does Specialized Medical offer?

Specialized Medical supports Holter Monitoring, Long-Term and Extended Holter Monitoring, Cardiac Event Monitoring and Mobile Cardiac Telemetry. The ordering physician selects the test and duration for each patient.

What is ambulatory cardiac monitoring?

Ambulatory cardiac monitoring records a patient's ECG while the patient continues ordinary activities outside a hospital. The physician chooses the service and monitoring duration based on the clinical question.

What does LIVE test-status visibility mean?

For supported live studies, Specialized Medical can follow operational indicators such as monitor battery, electrode contact, Bluetooth connection, cellular connection and successful ECG data transmission while the study is underway.

Are all Specialized Medical monitoring options LIVE STREAMING?

No. The primary Holter, longer Holter, Event and MCT options described as LIVE STREAMING include live test-status visibility. Selected non-live Holter configurations are also available for patients who are unable or unwilling to carry the assigned monitoring phone.

When are Holter findings presented to the ordering physician?

For Holter and Long-Term or Extended Holter studies, findings are presented after the final report is generated.

When are Event and MCT findings presented?

Qualifying findings from Cardiac Event Monitoring and Mobile Cardiac Telemetry may be presented during the study according to the physician-prescribed notification protocol, followed by a final report.

What happens if a live study loses an electrode or connection?

When a supported operational indicator falls outside the expected range, Specialized Medical can contact the patient and help troubleshoot the battery, electrode, phone, Bluetooth, cellular or transmission issue.

Which cellular networks support the assigned monitoring phone?

The assigned phone supports five cellular networks across two countries: AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon in the United States, and Telcel and AT&T Mexico in Mexico. Connectivity varies by location and network conditions, and uninterrupted coverage is not guaranteed.

Can Specialized Medical support post-TAVR monitoring?

Yes. When prescribed by the treating heart team, supported live monitoring services can be configured for post-discharge rhythm surveillance under the organization's patient-selection and notification protocol.

Does cardiac monitoring replace the treating physician's judgment?

No. Specialized Medical provides monitoring technology, operational support, review workflows and communication support. Test selection, interpretation and patient-care decisions remain with the treating physician and healthcare organization.
