Direct answer

What Is the S-Patch?

S-Patch is a compact wearable ECG monitor built around two small circular components connected by one thin lead. Specialized Medical uses the system to support multiple physician-prescribed ambulatory cardiac monitoring studies through one familiar equipment and reporting workflow.

In practical terms: practices can use one patient-friendly platform for short Holter studies, longer Holter studies, Cardiac Event Monitoring and Mobile Cardiac Telemetry instead of managing a different device workflow for every study type.

The monitor pairs with a dedicated, preconfigured phone. In supported live workflows, that phone automatically transmits ECG data through an available cellular network. Patients do not need to upload recordings manually or navigate a general-purpose smartphone.

Study path Typical duration How S-Patch supports it Clinical communication
Holter Monitoring 24–48 hours Continuous ECG acquisition with streamlined enrollment and disconnect. Results are provided in the final report after the recording is processed.
Long-Term Holter Monitoring More than 48 hours through 7 days Compact wear and a 10-day minimum battery specification support longer collection. Results are provided in the final report.

Study duration, notification thresholds and clinical use are determined by the prescribing physician. Live operational visibility is not the same as a diagnosis or guaranteed clinical outcome.

Designed for wearability

A Small Monitor That Stays Out of the Patient’s Way

The S-Patch configuration is intentionally simple: two low-profile disks, one connecting lead and a dedicated phone that handles transmission.

Two-component design

The disks sit on two instructed skin locations. The single lead can be routed beneath clothing to support a more discreet patient experience.

Lightweight for daily wear

At approximately 0.6 ounces, S-Patch is designed to minimize bulk while the patient moves through normal day-to-day activities.

Repositionable electrodes

Electrode positions can be changed on the skin as directed, helping avoid repeated stress on a single location while maintaining ECG acquisition.

Always Connected workflow

ECG Transmission Without Manual Uploading

The assigned monitoring phone connects to S-Patch over Bluetooth and transmits ECG data over a supported cellular network. The phone is dedicated to the study and keeps the patient experience intentionally simple.

Specialized Medical's Always Connected technology supports five cellular networks across two countries: AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon in the United States, and Telcel and AT&T Mexico in Mexico. This expanded connectivity helps maintain live ECG data transmission in supported monitoring configurations, subject to available coverage and network conditions.

Only two patient screens

Device Status and Log Symptoms—without web browsing, email, games or unnecessary settings.

Automatic transmission

Supported live workflows send ECG data as it is recorded, removing end-of-study batch uploads and related delays. See LIVE ECG monitoring.

Proactive support

Operational Issues Can Be Addressed While the Study Is Active

What happens next

Frequently asked questions

S-Patch System Answers

Clear answers for practices evaluating the wearable ECG system and service workflow.

S-Patch is a compact dual-disk wearable ECG monitor used by Specialized Medical for prescribed ambulatory studies. One thin lead connects the two components, and the system works with a dedicated cellular phone to transmit ECG data in supported live-monitoring workflows.

Specialized Medical uses the S-Patch platform to support Holter Monitoring, Long-Term Holter Monitoring, Extended Holter Monitoring, Cardiac Event Monitoring and Mobile Cardiac Telemetry (MCT), depending on the physician's order and the selected workflow.

The S-Patch system weighs approximately 0.6 ounces. Its two circular components are designed to be compact and unobtrusive during normal daily activity.

The current validated S-Patch battery specification is a minimum of 10 days per battery. A prescribed study may last up to 30 days; a study longer than the battery specification includes battery handling managed according to the prescribed test and support workflow. A single battery is not stated to last 30 days.

The S-Patch is rated IP55, which indicates resistance to dust and water splashes during everyday wear. IP55 is not a waterproof rating. Patients should follow the approved use and care instructions supplied with their prescribed study, including any guidance on showering and on repositioning electrodes, which should be done only according to those approved patient instructions.

In Specialized Medical's supported live-monitoring workflows, S-Patch ECG data is transmitted through a dedicated cellular phone to the monitoring platform. This removes manual batch uploading and lets the support team follow operational study status while the test is underway.

The preconfigured phone connects to the S-Patch by Bluetooth and sends ECG data over one of five supported cellular networks: AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon in the United States, and Telcel and AT&T Mexico in Mexico, subject to available coverage. Its patient-facing functions are intentionally limited to device status and symptom logging, helping keep the study workflow simple.

When operational indicators fall outside the expected range, Specialized Medical's support team can contact the patient and help troubleshoot the battery, electrode contact, Bluetooth connection, cellular connection or data transmission before additional monitoring time is lost.

No equipment purchase is required for Specialized Medical's program. The company supplies the monitoring equipment and routine study supplies needed for the agreed program, subject to the service agreement.

A physician practice, cardiology group, hospital or health system can request a demonstration or discuss a small no-obligation pilot with Specialized Medical. The team will confirm fit, workflow and implementation requirements before patient use.
