Direct answer

What is live ECG monitoring?

Live ECG monitoring transmits ambulatory rhythm information during the prescribed study instead of waiting until the wearable is returned. In the Specialized Medical workflow, the wearable monitor communicates with an assigned phone by Bluetooth. The phone sends available ECG information through cellular service into the monitoring workflow when connectivity is available.

Live transmission can support continuous ECG monitoring, remote ECG oversight and earlier visibility into qualifying Event or MCT findings. It does not mean that one person manually watches every beat or that every transmission and communication is instantaneous.

What happens after an interruption?

The system is designed to reconnect when service becomes available after a temporary interruption. Transmission still depends on monitor operation, Bluetooth, phone power and status, network availability and successful data receipt. Uninterrupted coverage and instantaneous transmission are not guaranteed.

Two distinct responsibilities

Operational visibility is not the same as clinical notification

Two separate teams support a live study. Clinical ECG monitoring personnel review ECG data and communicate qualifying findings according to the prescribed service. Proactive patient-support personnel identify technical interruptions, contact patients and help troubleshoot.

PATIENT-SUPPORT TEAM

Keeping the study connected

For supported live configurations, Specialized Medical can follow key technical indicators and assist the patient when a problem is identified.

  • Battery level
  • Electrode contact and quality
  • Monitor-to-phone connection
  • Phone-to-network connection
  • Successful ECG data receipt
  • Visible technical artifact
CLINICAL ECG MONITORING TEAM

Reviewing ECG data and communicating qualifying findings

Clinical communication depends on the ordered service and the physician-prescribed criteria, contacts and escalation pathway.

  • Event and MCT findings may be presented during the study
  • Communication follows the prescribed protocol
  • Telephone, text and email notifications where supported by the configured protocol
  • Authorized physicians can access ECG information through the secure portal
  • Holter findings are presented after the final report
  • The treating physician makes patient-care decisions
  • The final report supports physician review and interpretation

Live ECG monitoring is not emergency response

Specialized Medical provides diagnostic ambulatory monitoring. The service does not replace 911, emergency medical services or the treating physician's emergency instructions. Patients with urgent symptoms such as chest pain, severe shortness of breath or stroke symptoms should follow their emergency instructions immediately rather than waiting for a monitoring call.
