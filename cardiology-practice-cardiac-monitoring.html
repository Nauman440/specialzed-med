Direct answer

What does a turnkey cardiac monitoring program do for a cardiology practice?

It gives the practice a defined operating model for ambulatory monitoring—from the first patient order through the signed final report—without leaving staff to assemble equipment, support, reporting and escalation processes on their own.

The practical value: cardiologists retain control of the prescribed test, notification parameters and final interpretation while Specialized Medical supports implementation, active-study operations, patient troubleshooting and reporting workflow.

Repeatable practice workflow

From patient order to signed report

A documented sequence gives front-office staff, clinical staff and physicians a shared understanding of what happens at each step.

  1. Order and pre-enrollSelect the prescribed study and prepare patient information before the hookup visit when appropriate.
  2. Confirm the studyVerify test type, duration, physician instructions and notification contacts.
  3. Apply and connectAttach the monitor, assign the dedicated phone for live workflows and confirm readiness.
  4. Support the patientSpecialized Medical assists with operational battery, electrode and connectivity issues.
  5. Deliver the reportThe practice receives the applicable report under the ordered service workflow.
  6. Physician reviewThe physician reviews, interprets, dates and signs the final report when configured.

Operational visibility

Support the active study without shifting the burden back to your staff

Two separate teams support the active study. The clinical ECG monitoring team reviews ECG data—24/7 for applicable services such as Cardiac Event Monitoring and Mobile Cardiac Telemetry—and communicates qualifying findings according to the physician-defined notification protocol. The proactive patient technical-support team follows operational indicators such as battery status, electrode contact, monitor-to-phone connection, cellular connectivity and successful ECG transmission; when an indicator falls outside the expected range, it contacts the patient and helps troubleshoot.

Dedicated patient phone

A preconfigured phone keeps the patient experience limited to essential monitoring functions. Specialized Medical's Always Connected technology supports five cellular networks across two countries: AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon in the United States, and Telcel and AT&T Mexico in Mexico. This expanded connectivity helps maintain live ECG data transmission in supported monitoring configurations, subject to available coverage and network conditions.

Reporting and physician control

Make the final step easier to review and complete

The report workflow should preserve supporting ECG information while making the study easier for the ordering physician to review, interpret and sign.

Physician-ready reporting

  • Final report generated automatically at the end of the study where supported
  • Study summary and supporting ECG information
  • Symptomatic and asymptomatic context where available
  • Full Disclosure Holter reporting when ordered
  • Secure report-delivery workflow

Review and electronic signature

  • Report appears in the configured portal workflow
  • Physician reviews findings and supporting strips
  • Interpretation is entered through the enabled process
